Project Management

Renovation projects are notorious for their unexpected challenges and pitfalls. My project management services harness years of experience and my extensive professional network to bring efficiency and value to the complex home improvement process.

Anyone who has been through a home improvement project, whether large or small, understands the inherent challenges and inefficiencies. My project management service marries my residential renovation and design experience with a vast network of contractors and service providers to deliver an efficient and cost-effective experience.

My role is to identify potential bottlenecks before they become time-consuming and expensive roadblocks, keeping your project on track and within budget.

After many years of working with and observing contractors, we are in tune with how to manage the on-site work environment.

From insisting on proper prep work (did they take the time to cover the stairs?) to the small details that many don't consider (are they taking into account proper spacers when needed to keep cabinet and appliance doors from banging?) — nothing is left to chance.

Whether you are just painting your home's interior and exterior, renovating the kitchen, building an addition, or adding an ADU in the backyard, you will enjoy and appreciate the benefits of our Project Management Service.
